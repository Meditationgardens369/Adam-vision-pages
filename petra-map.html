What we are selling

Two hours on a mat.

Nobody can picture it until they have done it, and that is the whole marketing problem. So the first job is to show it.

You asked: once, or many times?

Not once. A series of 3 to 5.

The founder, Robin Erkel, recommends three to five sessions close together. She says only part of it happens on the mat and most of it comes up in daily life between sessions. People feel one session, and then many come back because each one goes deeper.

So it is not a switch you flip once and it is on forever. For us that is good news, because a series is a better thing to sell than a single ticket.

Words

Say this, never that.

Say

  • What it is like, in a guest's own words.
  • "Everyone has their own process."
  • "No experience needed."
  • That it can bring up emotions, and that she holds the room.
  • That it is a process, so come more than once.

Never

  • Promise that it heals an illness, anxiety or trauma.
  • Say it replaces a doctor or a therapist.
  • Promise that everyone feels something the first time.
  • Film a guest without a clear yes.
  • Push someone who is in a fragile place to come.
