How we get there

Four stages across the year. Each one is useful the day it goes live, so you are never sitting waiting for the whole thing to be finished.

Months
1 to 3

One inbox, one brain

This is the part that gives you your evenings back, and it is first for a reason.

  • Booking, Airbnb, WhatsApp, email and Instagram all landing in one place instead of five
  • Every enquiry answered inside a minute, in the guest's own language, at any hour
  • Directions, door codes, wifi and checkout times sending themselves at the right moment
  • Review requests going out after every stay, timed properly, without you remembering
  • The turnover schedule building itself from the checkouts and reaching the cleaners each week
  • One proper record of every property, owner, booking and guest you have
Months
4 to 6

The back office runs itself

The admin that eats your Sundays stops being something you do.

  • A guest reports a broken air conditioner, a job opens, the right man is messaged, the owner is told, and it is all on the record
  • Foreign guest details collected at booking, so the boletim de alojamento is ready inside the three working day window instead of being remembered late
  • Tourist tax handled property by property, because it depends which council each one sits in
  • A monthly report per property: nights sold, revenue, occupancy, what was spent, what the owner is owed
  • Guest invoices issued, and one clean file a month for your accountant
Months
7 to 9

You stop being the bottleneck

Now it starts bringing money in rather than only saving time.

  • Your own booking page that takes payment, so you are not renting only through the platforms
  • Early check-in, late checkout, a transfer, a hamper, a mid-stay clean, a table at the restaurant, all offered at the moment guests are actually willing to buy
  • Past guests invited back before each season opens, while they are still deciding
  • One morning page: arrivals, departures, turnovers, money owed, open jobs, decisions waiting on you. You can ask it questions in plain English.
  • One day a month producing a month of posts across every platform, with every message they generate landing in the same inbox as everything else
Months
10 to 12

An agency, not a person

  • Properties twenty-one through forty, without adding hours
  • The restaurant and the rentals finally feeding each other in both directions

The part you may not have spotted

The restaurant closes for two months in winter. You have always treated that as the quiet bit of the year.

Winter is also exactly when property owners sit down and decide who is managing their place next season.
