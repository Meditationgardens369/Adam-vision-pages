    Watch every cut

    Claude can cut a word in half, or keep a false start you wanted gone. Watch the whole video once, and ask it for the written transcript so you can read what stays.

    Check what is on screen

    Screen recordings can show client names, emails and private details. Ask Claude to scan for them and blur them, then check yourself before you publish.

    Start small

    Long videos take longer to process and use more of your plan. Do a two-minute clip first, then your real video. If Claude needs an account or a key for something, it should tell you before it starts.

    Use music you are allowed to use

    Claude can mix music under your voice, but the track has to be one you have the rights to. Tools and limits change, so check what Claude shows you today.
