    Where to keep a hand on the wheel

    It only works if you use it

    The more you record, the more it knows and the more it can do for you. A few notes in the first week is normal. The real value shows up after a few weeks of daily or weekly notes.

    Check how it files

    Open Obsidian after your first few notes and look at where things landed. If you don't like it, tell Claude Code in plain words and it will change how it files.

    Your notes live on your computer

    Obsidian keeps your vault as a folder on your own computer. When Claude Code reads or files a note, that text goes through Claude, so only add what you are comfortable sharing with it.

    Client and health details

    If you work with clients or patients, leave out names and private details unless you have their permission and have checked the rules where you work. Use first names or initials until you're sure.
