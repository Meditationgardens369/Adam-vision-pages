About a LEAP session

You arrive.

We begin with an introduction, so you know what is coming and can ask me anything.

You lie down.

Comfortably on a yoga mat, eyes closed, while music plays.

The energy moves.

I move my hands above your body. The only thing you have to do is relax and surrender.

Whatever comes, comes.

People often experience spontaneous movement, emotion, visuals, or a feeling of deep calm and insight.

You land.

We close with time to integrate. A session lasts about two hours in all.
