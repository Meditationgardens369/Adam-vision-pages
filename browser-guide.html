    Where to keep a hand on the wheel

    It works as you

    It uses your browser, with the sites you are signed in to. Keep it away from banking, payments and anything you can't undo, and say clearly what it must not do.

    Watch it, don't walk away

    Stay near the screen the first few times. Press Stop if it goes the wrong way, then tell it what you wanted.

    Never let it send or buy on its own

    Collecting and filling in is fine. Sending messages, posting, buying or submitting should always be your own click.

    Websites change

    If it gets stuck on a page, press Stop, say what you can see, and ask it to try a different way. Check the sources it gives you.
