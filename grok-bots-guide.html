    Where to keep a hand on the wheel

    Check every claim

    Bots can sound sure and be wrong. Open the links, and ask for evidence every time. If it can't show you, don't use it.

    Start with drafts only

    Let a bot research and draft first. Only let it post or send once you've seen several good results, and keep approving each one yourself.

    Keep private details out

    Don't paste client, patient or family details into a bot. Use first names or leave details out until you're sure how your data is handled.

    Watch the cost and your time

    Bots use credits or usage, and they take your attention to review. Start with one bot for a week before you add more.
