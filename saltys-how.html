    What I would build

    Four small pieces. Not a big overhaul.

    Each one stands alone. We start with whichever matters most to you and leave the rest until you want them.

    Piece one

    A front door people can find

    A simple website and a Google profile with good photos and your patients' own words. When someone searches for a chiropractor in Lagoa, they see a clinic they trust, and they can book in one tap.

    Piece two

    An assistant for the messages

    It answers the usual questions on WhatsApp and the website, in Portuguese or English, and offers free times from your real diary. Anything unusual comes to you.

    Piece three

    Reminders that actually go out

    A friendly message the day before each appointment, with a way to confirm or move it. Fewer empty slots, and nobody has to remember to send them.

    Piece four

    A way to bring people back

    Someone who has not been in for months gets a warm check-in from you. After all these years, this may be where the most hidden value in the clinic sits.

    For both of you

    One diary. Two practitioners.

    If Julie works alongside you, the same system works for her treatments too. Patients can choose either of you, or both, and neither of you has to run it.

    For you

    • Fewer calls to answer while you are with a patient
    • A diary that fills and reminds itself
    • Past patients gently invited back

    For Julie

    • Her sports therapy and massage sessions bookable online
    • Follow-ups after a treatment or a course of sessions
    • Her own regulars looked after, without her chasing them
    Shared: one list of patients, one diary, and one place where the messages land.
